# Watchlist

The categories `/radar` scans for. Add, remove or reword freely. The examples are search starting points, not the only things to find.

## 1. Megaproyectos e infraestructura
Ports, logistics corridors, railways, highways, airports, industrial parks and special economic zones.
- Examples: the Chancay port in Perú and its effect on regional trade routes; bioceanic corridors; the Metro de Bogotá; Ecuador and Colombia port expansions.

## 2. Política industrial
New industrial strategies, productive development plans, incentives, reindustrialisation and local-content rules.
- Examples: Brazil's Nova Indústria Brasil; Colombia's reindustrialisation policy; Perú's and Ecuador's productive development plans.

## 3. Inversión extranjera y comercio
Large foreign direct investment (FDI) announcements, trade agreements, tariffs and export promotion.
- Examples: Chinese, EU or US investment in the region; CAN, Mercosur and Pacific Alliance decisions; new free trade agreements.

## 4. Transición energética y minerales críticos
Renewables, green hydrogen, lithium, copper, electromobility and energy efficiency in industry.
- Examples: lithium in Bolivia and the "lithium triangle"; green hydrogen projects in Chile, Colombia and Brasil.

## 5. Industria verde y economía circular
Cleaner production, decarbonisation of industry, circular economy laws and sustainable industrial parks.

## 6. Agroindustria y cadenas de valor
Value addition in agriculture, food processing, coffee, cocoa, fisheries and forestry, and quality and standards.

## 7. Innovación, digitalización y MiPymes
Industry 4.0, digital transformation, startups, tech parks, financing for small firms and skills.

## 8. Organismos multilaterales y ONU
Announcements, reports and programmes from UNIDO (ONUDI), ECLAC (CEPAL), CAN, IDB (BID), CAF, World Bank, UNCTAD, ILO, UNDP and similar bodies.

## 9. Investigación y publicaciones
New research papers, flagship reports and policy briefs on industrial development in the region.
