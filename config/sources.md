# Trusted sources

Where `/radar` looks, in order of preference. Search widely, but only publish stories from these levels. Mark paywalled sources with `[pago]` and prefer an open source for the same story.

**Linking rule:** every headline links to the article itself on the publisher's own site. When a story is an official announcement, link the official source (government, central bank or organization) rather than a media write-up of it.

## Level 1 — Governments and official bodies

| Country | Presidency and ministries | Central bank and statistics | Investment and trade agencies |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Colombia** | Presidencia (presidencia.gov.co), MinCIT, MinHacienda, MinEnergía, DNP | Banco de la República, DANE | ProColombia, iNNpulsa, Bancóldex |
| **Perú** | gob.pe (Presidencia, PCM), PRODUCE, MINCETUR, MEF, MINEM | BCRP, INEI | ProInversión, PromPerú |
| **Bolivia** | Presidencia, Ministerio de Desarrollo Productivo y Economía Plural, Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas Públicas, Ministerio de Hidrocarburos y Energías | Banco Central de Bolivia, INE | YLB (Yacimientos de Litio Bolivianos) |
| **Ecuador** | Presidencia, Ministerio de Producción, Comercio Exterior, Inversiones y Pesca (MPCEIP), Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas, Ministerio de Ambiente y Energía | Banco Central del Ecuador, INEC | Invest Ecuador / ProEcuador |
| **Regional benchmarks** | Brasil: MDIC, BNDES · Chile: Ministerio de Economía, CORFO · México: Secretaría de Economía | — | — |

### Official and public news agencies (Level 1 for official announcements)

- **Perú:** Agencia Peruana de Noticias **Andina** (andina.pe)
- **Bolivia:** Agencia Boliviana de Información — **ABI** (abi.bo)
- **Ecuador:** El Telégrafo (public media), official Presidencia and ministry press rooms
- **Colombia:** RTVC Noticias, Presidencia press room
- **Brasil:** Agência Brasil · **Chile:** official government portals (gob.cl)

These agencies are reliable for what governments announce. For criticism or independent context, pair them with Level 3 media.

## Level 2 — International and regional organizations

- UNIDO / ONUDI (unido.org)
- CEPAL / ECLAC (cepal.org), Comunidad Andina — CAN (comunidadandina.org)
- BID / IDB (iadb.org), CAF (caf.com), World Bank (worldbank.org), IMF (imf.org)
- UNCTAD, OIT / ILO, PNUD / UNDP, OECD, FAO, IRENA
- EU delegations and development agencies (GIZ, AFD, AECID, KOICA, JICA) for cooperation news

## Level 3 — Established national and international media

- **Colombia:** Portafolio, La República, El Tiempo, El Espectador, Semana, Valora Analitik, El Heraldo, El Colombiano
- **Perú:** Gestión, El Comercio, RPP, La República, Infobae Perú
- **Bolivia:** El Deber, Los Tiempos, La Razón, Opinión, Visión 360, Unitel
- **Ecuador:** Primicias, El Universo, Expreso, El Comercio, Extra
- **International wires and regional media:** Reuters, EFE, AFP, AP, Bloomberg, Bloomberg Línea, El País América, Infobae, Americas Quarterly, BNamericas `[pago]`, Dialogue Earth
- **Brasil:** Valor Econômico `[pago]`, Folha de S.Paulo, O Globo, Estadão

## Level 4 — Specialized trade press and industry associations

Accepted **only when another trusted source confirms the story**.

- Mining and energy: Rumbo Minero, Minería y Proyectos, Energiminas, Minería en Línea
- Ports and logistics: PortalPortuario, DataPortuaria
- Business associations: ANDI (Colombia), SNI (Perú), CNI (Brasil), CCL (Cámara de Comercio de Lima), Cámaras de Industrias (Bolivia, Ecuador)

## Never used

- Aggregators, search results, Google News, redirect links or content farms
- Opinion columns and editorials (except as context in research notes)
- Sponsored or advertorial content ("espacio empresarial", "contenido patrocinado", "publirreportaje")
- Unverified social media posts, anonymous sites, or sites that cannot be opened to verify
